## Reading it

Both approaches give the job to Dana, first on her remaining route, and the fleet totals differ by 0.7 min (124.3 vs 123.6). Here "don't reorder" costs little for the new job itself. VROOM's edge comes from swapping the order of Yossi's jobs 4 and 5 and Avi's jobs 7 and 8, which have nothing to do with the new job; Dana's route is identical in both. So the 0.7 min is mostly the price of leaving unrelated stops in their old order, not of the insertion. I ran a single scenario and did not separate the two effects by solving without the new job.
